1. Stage 1 (MRI): 3-Class Stroke Classification stroke_cnn_model.h5

Why We Cascade from Stage-1 MRI to Stage-2 MRI for Ischemic Stroke: Stage-1 MobileNetV2 achieves 100.0% Precision on Ischemic Stroke (FP = 0) with 92.68% Peak Validation Accuracy, making it a zero-false-alarm gatekeeper. However, its 2D slice recall on subtle infarcts is 67.0% and whole-image classification cannot measure infarct volume (mL), hemisphere/territory, or Core vs. Penumbra.

2. Stage 2 (MRI): Ischemic Lesion Segmentation & Volumetry best_stroke_model.keras

How Stage-2 MRI Resolves the Localization Gap: Trained on ISLES 2022 3D NIfTI (ADC/DWI) with 2D U-Net++ Deep Supervision (9.04M params), achieving 93.05% Peak Training Dice, 81.12% Peak Validation Dice, and >99.2% Voxel Specificity while extracting 5 clinical biomarkers (Axial Slice Depth, Vascular Territory, Area cm² & 3D Volume mL, Core p>0.80 vs Penumbra 0.30–0.60).

3. Stage 1 (CT): 3-Class CT Classification stroke_cnn_model_v2.keras

Why We Cascade ONLY Hemorrhagic Stroke to CT Stage 2: On External Hospital Validation (N=639, 77.46% Acc), Hemorrhage achieves 95.0% Recall (237/250), 88.0% Precision, and 91.0% F1 (and 87.0% Precision on Internal Test N=3,000) because acute blood is hyperdense (60–80 HU) on CT, whereas early Ischemia has lower precision on CT (61–65%, handled by MRI). However, Stage-1 CT cannot identify which of the 5 anatomical hemorrhage compartments is bleeding or quantify bleed area %.

4. Stage 2 (CT): NeuroFocus AI — 5-Subtype Classifier & U-Net Segmenter classifier_best.pth + unet_resnet34_ctich_best.pth

How Stage-2A (ResNet-50) & Stage-2B (ResNet-34 U-Net) Work Together: Stage-2A classifies 5 anatomical bleed subtypes across 101,986 slices with 96.8% Mean Binary Accuracy and 74.03% Any-Hemorrhage F1 (led by IVH 80.3% Precision / 75.1% F1 and IPH 74.2% Precision / 70.0% F1). For rare Epidural bleeds (EDH, N=89, 33.7% recall), Stage-2B ResNet-34 U-Net (72.26% Peak Validation Dice @ 0.55 threshold) + Decoder Grad-CAM segments the exact hyperdense bleed pixels in parallel so no hemorrhage is missed.
